Business Model Canvas

InsightO is an Enterprise EdTech SaaS platform that digitizes academic performance evaluation, enabling universities to collect, analyze, and act on structured feedback across all stakeholders.

🤝
Key Partners
  • Universities & Higher-Education Institutions (B2B)
  • Cloud Infrastructure & AI/ML Service Providers
  • Academic Accreditation Bodies
  • Educational Influencers & Content Creators (B2C)
⚙️
Key Activities
  • SaaS Platform Development (Web & Mobile Apps)
  • B2B Direct Sales & Institution Onboarding
  • B2C Digital Marketing & User Acquisition
  • AI Model Integration for Submission Analysis
  • Secure Self-Registration & Role Management
✨
Value Propositions
⚡ All-in-One Platform 🚀 Unified Web & Mobile 🤖 Embedded AI Intelligence 🔒 Anonymous & Secure ✅ Accreditation Compliance
  • All-in-One Ecosystem: Replaces fragmented tools by consolidating course evaluations, survey creation, real-time analytics, and reporting into a single platform.
  • Native Multi-Role Workflows: Tailored, seamless experience for Students, Instructors, Admins, and Quality Officers across Web & Mobile apps.
  • Built-in AI Analytics: Instant automated sentiment analysis and actionable qualitative feedback without requiring external data tools.
  • Accreditation Compliance: Ready-to-use academic compliance metrics, audit trails, and strict anonymous feedback protocols.
💬
Customer Relationships

  • B2C: Automated self-service onboarding and community support.
  • Continuous platform updates and AI feature releases.
